Real quick

Can I lease a beat?

No. No leases, no tiers, no stream caps. Buy it outright or don't.

What files do I get?

Exactly what's listed on that beat's own card and row — same list in your order and agreement.

How does first come, first served work?

Simple: whoever buys it first gets it. The second it sells, it's pulled from the store for good — never sold to anyone else. Your agreement is the proof.

Payment plans?

Yes — Afterpay, Klarna and Affirm. Beats up to $2,000 split into 4 interest-free payments with Afterpay (price ÷ 4 — every Vault row shows its exact number). Bigger beats, up to $4,000, go on Afterpay monthly installments over 6 or 12 months (interest applies). Above $4,000 there's no payment plan — full price up front. US buyers, subject to approval. First come, first served still rules: once your order is confirmed, the beat is pulled for good.

Are beats available now?

Yes — beats in the Vault are for sale now. First come, first served — once a beat sells, it's pulled from the store for good. Drop your email in the first-word signup on this page to get first word on what's in the Vault.

Publishing splits?

Set per song on the split sheet that comes with your sale — filled in for your record in the documents.
