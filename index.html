Real quick

Can I lease a beat?

No. No leases, no tiers, no stream caps. Buy it outright or don't.

What files do I get?

Exactly what's on the card. WAV-only beats deliver the WAV master. Full-file beats deliver MP3 + WAV + stems — same list in your order and agreement.

How does first come, first served work?

Simple: whoever buys it first gets it. The second it sells, it's pulled from the store for good — never sold to anyone else. Your agreement is the proof.
